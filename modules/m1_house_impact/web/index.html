4. Honest limitations

  • Labels are flood exposure (distance of each building to SAR-detected flood water on 2 Dec 2025), not structural damage grades. Sentinel-2 (10 m) cannot see roof damage.
  • The SAR pass was ~1 day after the Kelani peak (receding flood) and SAR misses water between dense buildings (double bounce), so some exposed buildings are labelled Low.
  • One event and one basin: the model must be re-validated on another flood (e.g. May 2016 / 2024) before operational use. The M4 3-day forecast input is not yet integrated.
